straight, minimal, correct:

classic deviled eggs

ingredients
	•	6 large eggs
	•	3 tbsp mayo
	•	1.5 tsp dijon (or yellow if that truly is all you have, but dijon is better)
	•	1–2 tsp white vinegar or lemon juice (taste it)
	•	1/8 tsp kosher salt (then adjust)
	•	1/8 tsp black pepper (fine grind)
	•	paprika for garnish

method
	1.	put eggs in pot, cover with cold water by ~1 inch.
	2.	bring pot to a full rolling boil, then immediately kill heat, lid on, and let sit 11–12 minutes.
	3.	ice bath 5 minutes.
	4.	peel under cold running water.
	5.	halve, pop yolks into bowl.
	6.	fork mash yolks → mayo → mustard → acid → salt → pepper. mix until creamy (no chalk).
	7.	taste it now. if it’s dull, it’s almost always acid or salt. adjust until it hits.
	8.	pipe or spoon back into whites.
	9.	dust paprika right before serving.

notes if you care about doing this well
	•	the creamy texture is 90% emulsification + enough acid. most “mid” deviled eggs are simply under-acided.
	•	optional: 1 tsp pickle brine is excellent and replaces some or all vinegar.
	•	optional upgrade: microplane a tiny amount of fresh onion into the yolk base (seriously: like 1/4 tsp). it gives depth without onion flavor.

if serving more than 20 minutes later, keep the filling in a piping bag in the fridge and pipe right before service — the cut whites dry out exposed.
