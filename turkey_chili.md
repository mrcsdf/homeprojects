# Turkey Chili with Fresh Tomatoes + Two Salsas (No Tomato Paste)

A rich, concentrated chili built from **hard-browned turkey**, **fresh vine tomatoes**, and a **blend of mild salsa + Trader Joe’s Fall Harvest salsa**. No tomato paste needed—just time and proper browning.

## Ingredients

- **Oil:** 1–2 tbsp
- **Onion:** 1 medium, diced
- **Bell pepper:** 1, diced
- **Garlic:** 3–4 cloves, minced
- **Ground turkey:** 1 lb
- **Vine tomatoes:** ~2–2½ lb (about 6–8 medium), chopped
- **Mild salsa:** ½ cup
- **TJ Fall Harvest salsa:** ½ cup  
  - **Total salsa:** 1 cup  
  - For a sweeter/more “fall” vibe: **¾ cup Fall Harvest + ¼ cup mild**
- **Broth or water:** ½–1 cup (start with ½ cup; add more if needed)

### Spices (toast in the pot)
- **Chili powder:** to taste (start with 2 tbsp if you need a baseline)
- **Cumin:** to taste (start with 1–2 tsp)
- **Oregano:** to taste (start with 1 tsp)
- **Paprika:** to taste (start with 1 tsp)
- **Cayenne:** optional pinch

### Beans (choose one)
- Traditional: your usual chili beans  
- OR **Cannellini + garbanzos (works great)**  
  - Cannellini = creamy/thickening  
  - Garbanzos = firm/holds shape  
  - If mixing: **2 parts cannellini : 1 part garbanzo** is a great ratio  
  - If canned: **rinse + drain**

### Salt + finish
- **Salt:** add lightly early, adjust at the end (salsa varies wildly)
- **Lime juice or vinegar:** optional splash at the end if it tastes flat

## Instructions

1. **Sauté aromatics**  
   Heat oil in a large pot over medium heat. Sauté onion + pepper **6–8 min** until softened. Add garlic and cook **30 sec**.

2. **Brown the turkey hard (key flavor step)**  
   Add ground turkey and cook until **deeply browned**. Let it sit and develop color—don’t rush. This replaces the flavor you’d normally get from tomato paste.

3. **Toast spices (1 minute)**  
   Add chili powder, cumin, oregano, paprika (and optional cayenne). Stir constantly for **~1 min** until fragrant.

4. **Add tomatoes + salsa**  
   Stir in chopped vine tomatoes + both salsas. Add **½ cup broth/water** to start (add more later only if needed).

5. **Simmer uncovered (45–60 minutes)**  
   Simmer **uncovered**, stirring occasionally, until it looks like **chili—not soup**. This concentrates the fresh tomatoes.

6. **Add beans after the base is cooking down**  
   Add beans around **20–30 minutes into the simmer** (so they don’t get mushy).  
   - For **cannellini/garbanzos**, lean later: **~30–35 min into simmer**, then cook **15–25 min more**.

7. **Thicken if needed (pick one)**  
   - Mash **½ cup beans** against the pot wall (works especially well with cannellini), **or**
   - Add **2 handfuls crushed tortilla chips**, simmer **10 min**, **or**
   - Stir in **1 tbsp masa harina + 2 tbsp water**, simmer **10 min**

8. **Salt last + adjust acidity**  
   Start with **½ tsp salt early**, then adjust at the end.  
   Add a splash of **lime juice or vinegar** only if it tastes flat.

## Notes on TJ Fall Harvest Salsa

It can lean sweeter/spiced. That’s fine in chili, but don’t overdo it unless you want a subtle “autumn chili” vibe. **½ cup is the safe zone.**

## Quick Taste Fixes

- **Too sweet:** add more chili powder + a squeeze of lime
- **Too acidic:** add a pinch of sugar *or* a dollop of sour cream in the bowl
- **Too thin:** use a thickener (Step 7) + simmer longer

## Why This Works Without Tomato Paste

The chili gets depth from:
- **hard browning** the turkey
- **uncovered simmering** to reduce and concentrate fresh tomatoes
- **toasting spices** directly in the pot
