Perfect. Use the vine tomatoes as the main “tomato base” and use the salsas as your acidity + seasoning booster. You’ll get a legit chili, just keep an eye on salt because salsa brings it.

Do-this-now plan (6 servings)

Use
	•	Vine tomatoes: ~2–2½ lb (about 6–8 medium), chopped
	•	Mild salsa: ½ cup
	•	TJ Fall Harvest salsa: ½ cup
(Total salsa 1 cup. If you want it sweeter/more “fall,” push Fall Harvest to ¾ cup and mild to ¼ cup.)

Steps (what changes vs my original)
	1.	Sauté onion + pepper in oil 6–8 min. Add garlic 30 sec.
	2.	Brown turkey well (this is where flavor comes from if you’re skipping paste).
	3.	Toast spices 1 min: chili powder + cumin + oregano + paprika (and a pinch of cayenne if you want).
	4.	Add tomatoes + salsa:
	•	Stir in chopped vine tomatoes + both salsas.
	•	Add ½–1 cup broth/water (start with ½; you can add more).
	5.	Simmer uncovered 45–60 min, stirring occasionally, until it looks like chili and not soup.
	6.	Beans go in after 20–30 min of simmering (so they don’t get mushy).
	7.	Thicken if needed (choose one):
	•	Mash ½ cup beans against the pot wall, OR
	•	Add 2 handfuls crushed tortilla chips, simmer 10 min, OR
	•	If you have it: 1 tbsp masa harina + 2 tbsp water, stir in, simmer 10 min.
	8.	Salt last. Start with ½ tsp early, then adjust at the end because salsa varies wildly. Finish with a splash of lime/vinegar only if it tastes flat (you might not need it because salsa already has acid).

Notes on the TJ Fall Harvest salsa

It can lean sweeter/spiced. That’s fine in chili, but don’t overdo it unless you want a subtle “autumn chili” vibe. Keeping it to ½ cup is the safe zone.

Quick taste-fix cheats
	•	Too sweet: add more chili powder + a squeeze of lime.
	•	Too acidic: add a pinch of sugar or a dollop of sour cream in the bowl.
	•	Too thin: thicken (step 7) + simmer longer.

This will work nicely with zero paste. The key is browning the turkey hard and uncovered simmer time to concentrate the vine tomatoes.
