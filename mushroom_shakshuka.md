Copycat Tatte Potato, Mushroom & Bacon “Shakshuka”

Serves: 2 (big portions)

Ingredients

Base (potato cream sauce)
	•	1 tbsp butter
	•	1/2 yellow onion or 1 shallot, diced
	•	250–300 g Yukon gold potatoes, peeled, 1-inch cubes
	•	2 cups (480 ml) chicken or veg broth
	•	1/2 cup (120 ml) heavy cream
	•	1 tsp paprika
	•	1/2 tsp garlic powder (or 1 minced garlic clove)
	•	Salt + black pepper

(That potato+broth+cream approach is a proven “Tatte-inspired” method.)  ￼

Mushrooms + greens
	•	1 tbsp olive oil
	•	250–350 g mushrooms (mix shiitake + button if you can), sliced
	•	Big handful baby spinach (2–3 cups)
	•	Salt + pepper

Eggs + bacon
	•	4 slices bacon
	•	2–4 eggs (depending how egg-heavy you want it)

Toppings (make it taste like the restaurant)
	•	1/4 cup grated parmesan
	•	Fresh parsley

Parsley relish
	•	1/2 cup chopped parsley
	•	1 tbsp olive oil
	•	1 tbsp lemon juice
	•	1 tbsp finely minced shallot (optional)
	•	Pinch salt

Garlic Aleppo oil
	•	3 tbsp olive oil
	•	1 small garlic clove, thinly sliced
	•	1–2 tsp Aleppo pepper (or mild chili flakes)
	•	Pinch salt

Serve with toasted sourdough.

⸻

Steps
	1.	Cook bacon: In a skillet, cook bacon until crisp. Remove; keep 1–2 tbsp bacon fat in the pan (optional but good).
	2.	Make potato cream sauce:
Sauté onion in butter (or bacon fat) 3–4 min. Add potatoes + broth, bring to a boil, cover and simmer until potatoes are very tender (~10 min). Blend until smooth (immersion blender = easiest). Stir in cream + paprika + garlic powder. Season aggressively with salt/pepper.  ￼
	3.	Brown mushrooms: In the same (or a second) pan, heat olive oil. Add mushrooms + salt; cook until they release water and then brown (8–12 min). Add spinach and wilt 30–60 sec.
	4.	Combine: Stir mushrooms + spinach into the potato cream sauce. Adjust thickness with a splash of broth or cream.
	5.	Eggs (two ways):
	•	Most “shakshuka-style”: Make wells, crack eggs in, cover, cook gently until whites set (5–8 min).
	•	Most “Tatte-style”: Poach eggs separately and place on top right before serving. (Their descriptions call them poached.)  ￼
	6.	Finish: Crumble bacon over, add parmesan, parsley relish, and drizzle garlic Aleppo oil.

⸻

Quick tweaks to nail the vibe
	•	Make the potato sauce very smooth (blend longer than you think).
	•	Don’t under-salt the base; potatoes mute seasoning.
	•	For the Aleppo oil: warm oil + garlic just until garlic barely turns pale gold, then kill heat and stir in Aleppo.
